Khmer human speech used in iSpeak Confidence V16.0.2

Source package supplied to the project:
Common Voice Scripted Speech 26.0 — Khmer (km), corpus dated 2026-06-12.

Selection method:
- Read validated.tsv from the supplied corpus.
- Kept only recordings present in the supplied clips directory.
- Kept one recording per exact NFC-normalized Khmer transcript.
- When the same transcript had multiple validated recordings, retained one and removed duplicate transcript entries from the in-app pathway.
- 121 unique validated Khmer sentence recordings are bundled in this build.
- Course vocabulary audio uses exact transcript matching only. If no exact human recording exists, Khmer synthetic speech is not substituted by the course audio helper.
