An Android App Bundle cannot be honestly called upload-ready until Android Studio/SDK 36 compiles the release and the signed build is exercised on a real Android device. The supplied environment does not contain the Android SDK, so no AAB was fabricated or falsely claimed as tested.

Google Play policy note
The app's paid 1:1 tutoring flow should be reviewed against the current Google Play Payments policy before production. Google's current exception for 1:1 online paid services requires the service to be between two individuals and not be available for replay afterwards in a Play-distributed app. iSpeak currently contains disclosed classroom recording/admin-recording functionality, so this deserves a deliberate policy decision rather than an unrequested functional change.
