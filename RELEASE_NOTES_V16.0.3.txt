iSpeak Confidence V16.0.3 — Khmer Beginner Entry Repair

KHMER PLACEMENT
- Beginner Khmer placement now starts with supplied romanised forms such as Baat, Chah, Sous'dey, Chum reap sour, Soum, Ar-kun and Lea haey.
- Questions 1-8 deliberately support learners who can speak some Khmer but cannot yet read Khmer script independently.
- Questions 9-14 now contain guaranteed real-human Khmer listening from exact Common Voice clips bundled in the project.
- Listening questions hide the transcript until the learner chooses from complete Khmer sentence options.
- Later placement progressively removes romanisation and moves into Khmer reading and independent written production.
- The test remains 30 questions with increasing difficulty.

KHMER FOUNDATIONS
- Added a visible Khmer Script & Spoken Foundations entry at the top of the Khmer Learn pathway.
- Added an essential spoken-Khmer starter panel with Khmer script + English-letter reading + English meaning.
- Added the 33 supplied Khmer consonants.
- Added the supplied dependent vowels.
- Added supplied consonant + dependent-vowel combination examples.
- Added direct access from Foundations to the full Writing Academy.

AUDIO SAFETY
- Khmer placement listening uses exact bundled human Common Voice recordings.
- Normal Khmer lesson audio still refuses to substitute a mismatched or synthetic recording.
