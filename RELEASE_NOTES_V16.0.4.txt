iSpeak Confidence V16.0.4 — Advanced Khmer Conversation Progression

KHMER PLACEMENT
- Q1–8 remain beginner-friendly with Khmer script plus English-letter romanisation.
- Q9–14 are verified human Common Voice listening questions.
- Q15–20 bridge from supported reading into independent Khmer reading.
- Q21–30 are now progressively harder real-world conversation tasks rather than simple translation prompts.
- The final ten cover changed plans, reasoned opinions, past experiences, consequences/advice, polite disagreement, clarification, balanced advice, complaints/negotiation, persuasion/compromise and a very advanced multi-part conversation.
- Advanced responses have no single memorised answer. Gemini evaluates communicative task completion, connected Khmer, detail and appropriateness.
- Q30 requires reconstructing a complicated situation, clarifying intent, taking responsibility where appropriate, disagreeing politely, proposing a fair solution and asking a relevant follow-up question.

KHMER COURSE
- Units 21–30 now add a progressively harder advanced conversation challenge to the Sentence Builder, Patterns/Grammar and AI Conversation blocks.
- Advanced tasks require connected responses, reasons, consequences, clarification, politeness, negotiation and independent communication.
- Romanisation/model answers are deliberately not supplied inside these advanced challenge blocks.
- Beginner course support remains unchanged; advanced difficulty is introduced only in the later course.

IMPORTANT
- No new learner-facing Khmer phrases were invented for this change. Advanced scenarios/instructions are English task prompts; the learner supplies the Khmer.
- Existing supplied Khmer curriculum material remains the source of learner-facing Khmer teaching content.
