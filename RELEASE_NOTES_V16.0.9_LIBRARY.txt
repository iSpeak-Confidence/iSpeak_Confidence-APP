iSpeak Confidence V16.0.9 — Relaxed Digital Library

Added:
- Language-aware Library accessible from Home and the side menu.
- Shelf automatically follows the learner's selected course language.
- Curated famous classics for English, French, Spanish, Mandarin, Japanese and Arabic.
- Reading opens the verified public-domain/open source host in a new tab, preserving source/licence information.
- No quizzes, XP requirements or forced exercises: this is intentionally relaxed reading.
- Responsive book cards for phone/bedtime browsing.
- Khmer shelf deliberately remains unfilled until an exact user-approved/legal Khmer edition is verified; no Khmer learner text was invented.

Rights approach:
- Standard Ebooks editions used for the English shelf are CC0 as to Standard Ebooks' ebook files, while Standard Ebooks warns underlying public-domain status can vary outside the U.S.
- Wikisource states public-domain works may be reused for any reason; licensed material remains subject to its stated licence.
- The app links to source editions rather than copying uncertain translations into the bundle.
- This is the conservative implementation for a potentially commercial/global app.
