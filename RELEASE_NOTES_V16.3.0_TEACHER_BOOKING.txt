iSpeak Confidence V16.3.0 — Tutor Booking & Teacher Portal

- Replaced free-text preferred time with live selectable tutor availability.
- Added 14-day booking calendar and available time slots.
- Selected slots are held immediately to prevent double-booking.
- Added Teacher Portal from the Teachers page.
- Teachers can manage weekly availability and booking statuses.
- Booking data stored in data/teachers.json.
- Teacher portal protected by TEACHER_PORTAL_PIN (development default 2468; change before launch).
- Booking email notifications remain supported.
