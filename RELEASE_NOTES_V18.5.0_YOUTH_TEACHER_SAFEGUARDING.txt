iSpeak Confidence V18.5.0 — Youth Teacher Safeguarding & Student Conduct

- Removed the previous 18+ only teacher-application requirement.
- Teacher applicants can select 15–17 or 18+.
- Applicants aged 15–17 must confirm parent/legal-guardian approval before submission.
- The Admin application review shows age group and guardian-consent status.
- Students must accept professional classroom conduct rules before entering an iSpeak Classroom.
- Rules prohibit harassment, sexual/romantic behaviour, requests for private contact/payment details, off-platform meetings, and unauthorized recording/screenshots.
- Terms of Use updated with student conduct and youth-teacher safeguarding sections.
- Browser cache version bumped so the new form and classroom notice load immediately.
