iSpeak Confidence V18.6.1 — Library / Hero Series

- Library now opens to a real two-path hub instead of dumping learners into the classic catalogue.
- iSpeak Hero Series is the primary original reading experience; Classic Library is retained separately.
- Seven official heroes are presented as collections with two connected volumes each (14 books per selected language).
- Volume 2 is explicitly marked as a sequel and follows Volume 1.
- Hero covers use character art, layered backgrounds, title treatment, volume badges and sequel treatment instead of emoji placeholders.
- Hero collection cards expose both book titles before opening the shelf.
- English uses 12 chapters per volume; Chinese, Spanish, French, Japanese and Arabic use 10; Khmer uses 8.
- Khmer story text remains deliberately locked until approved Khmer learner text is available.
- Classic books keep the existing internal reader, bookmarks, progress, contents, notes, day/night and typography controls.
- Reader Back returns to Classic Library; Library navigation returns to the hub.
- Desktop/tablet/mobile responsive rules added for the new hub and Hero Series.
