iSpeak Confidence V18.8.45 — Closed Beta Android Build

This release carries forward the V18.8.44 closed-beta fixes and hardens Android audio end-to-end.

Included:
- Android/WebView writing-canvas Master & next fix.
- Admin-controlled student lesson price and teacher pay.
- Native Android TextToSpeech bridge for learning audio.
- All remaining synthesized IELTS/listening/diagnostic/mock fallbacks now route through the native Android TTS bridge first.
- Browser/PWA speech synthesis remains as fallback outside the Play build.
- Khmer learning audio continues to use exact verified human recordings where mapped; it is not replaced by synthetic Khmer speech.
- Android package remains com.ispeakconfidence.app.
- Play versionCode remains 2 because versionCode 1 is the existing closed-test build and V18.8.44 was not uploaded.
- versionName 18.8.45.

Real-device closed-beta validation required after upload:
1. Open a Latin writing exercise, draw the requested character, tap Master & next repeatedly.
2. Play learning audio in multiple non-Khmer languages.
3. Play IELTS Listening, diagnostic and full mock audio.
4. Confirm microphone/camera permission prompts.
5. Confirm admin can change teacher student-price/pay and that student-facing booking uses the saved student price.
