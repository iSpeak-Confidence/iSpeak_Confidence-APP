iSpeak Confidence V18.8.50 — Mobile + IELTS + full release hardening

Fixes completed in this build:
- Learning-language selection now retries Android scrolling after the course renders and lands on the learner's first/current incomplete unit.
- Both Writing Academy and quick Writing Practice canvases now have Android touch fallbacks as well as pointer handling.
- Android keeps the same WebView/state across rotation instead of recreating the app and returning to Home.

- Android capture-phase routing covers IELTS mode, level, course unit, skill and block controls.
- IELTS still contains 6 levels × 12 units = 72 units × 8 blocks = 576 study blocks.
