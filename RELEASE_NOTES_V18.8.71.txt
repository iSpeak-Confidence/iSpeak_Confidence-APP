iSpeak Confidence V18.8.71 — CAMERA + MICROPHONE RELIABILITY

- Fixes the classroom main WebRTC negotiation race that could leave both participants unable to see or hear each other while screen sharing still worked.
- Makes teacher-led audio/video negotiation deterministic.
- Adds a unique negotiation ID to offers, answers and ICE candidates so stale signaling from a replaced connection is ignored.

- Adds support for multiple TURN relay endpoints through TURN_URLS, with TURN_URL retained for backward compatibility.
