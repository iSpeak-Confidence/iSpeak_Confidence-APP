iSpeak Confidence Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026

This page describes the current app behaviour. The policy and any age/consent flow should be reviewed for the countries and app stores where iSpeak Confidence is released.

Information stored on your device

Guest learning progress, settings, lesson requests and profile details can be stored in the browser on the device so the app can remember progress.

Accounts and cloud progress

If you create an account, the server stores your email address, a salted password hash, account/session information and the learning progress you choose to synchronize. Passwords are not stored in plain text.

Microphone, speech and AI features

Microphone access is requested only when you start a speaking feature. When server speech transcription, AI conversation or AI voice features are used, the relevant text or audio is sent to the configured AI provider (currently Google Gemini) to provide that feature. Do not submit unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

Tutor requests and email

If you request a tutor lesson, the details you enter may be emailed to iSpeak Confidence so the request can be arranged. If direct server email is unavailable, the app may open your device email application with the request prepared for you to send.

Teacher applications and teacher profiles

If you apply to teach, iSpeak Confidence may store the information you submit, including your name, contact details, country, timezone, languages, teaching background, education, availability, requested rate, profile photo, introduction video and qualification/certificate files. Application materials are used to review your application and, if approved, selected profile information may be displayed to learners. Qualification files are treated as private review documents and are not intended to be publicly listed.

Approved teachers may also have scheduling information and lesson-booking records stored so they can manage availability and classes. Applicants and teachers should avoid uploading unnecessary sensitive personal information.

Retention and deletion

Guest data remains on the device until browser/app storage is cleared. Signed-in users can delete their account and synchronized learning data from Profile & Settings → Account & Cloud Sync. Teacher-application records and uploaded application materials may be retained while an application is under review or while a teacher account is active, and should be deleted when no longer needed subject to legitimate operational or legal requirements. Operational backups or host-level retention, if enabled in production, must be documented before release.

Children

If the service is offered to children, age-appropriate consent, parental controls and app-store requirements must be configured for the target market before release.

Security

Reasonable technical controls are used, including password hashing, request limits and security headers. No online service can guarantee absolute security.
